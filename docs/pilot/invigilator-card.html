Invigilator card: practice exam on PRIMERS OS

PRIMERS OS v1.0.0-rc1 · Veritas pilot

1Before candidates arrive (15 min)

  1. On the invigilator's PC, open a terminal: right-click the desktop › Terminal.
  2. Start the exam on every lab PC. Type the line the exam officer wrote below, for example: sudo primers-fleet exam-start https://cbt.example.edu.ng/login --allow cdn.jsdelivr.net Exam line:
  3. Check every PC: sudo primers-fleet status
    Each PC must say it is reachable, exam on, nobody signed in.
  4. Walk round: every screen shows the login screen with the shared lab account.

2Candidates

  • Click the shared lab account, then Log In. No password.
  • The exam opens full screen. Nothing else can be opened; that is normal.
  • The bar at the top says the exam is logged (pages opened, blocked pages, sign-in and finish times).
  • When finished: Finish exam (top right). The PC goes back to the login screen and forgets everything.

3Watching the room

sudo primers-fleet status --watch 10 Updates every 10 seconds: who is signed in, the last thing each PC did. Press Ctrl+C to stop watching (the exam carries on).

!A PC loses power or restarts

  1. Write down the seat and the time.
  2. Switch it back on. It comes back still in the exam, locked.
  3. The candidate signs in again and carries on. What they had already sent to the exam site is kept by the exam site.

!A PC freezes (no response for 1 minute)

  1. Write down the seat and the time.
  2. Hold the power button until it switches off (about 5 seconds), then switch it on. Then as above.
  3. Not back within 3 minutes? Move the candidate to a spare PC (spares are in the exam too) and write down both seats.

!The exam page doesn't load, or shows an error

  • One PC: check its network cable, then restart it as for a freeze. Several PCs: check sudo primers-fleet status and call IT. Don't stop the exam for everyone.
  • If the invigilator's PC fails: on a lab PC with nobody on it, sign in with the staff account and use sudo primers-exam status, sudo primers-exam stop on that PC.

4At the end

  1. Ask everyone to press Finish exam. Wait until sudo primers-fleet status shows nobody signed in.
  2. Stop the exam on every PC: sudo primers-fleet exam-stop (it would sign out anyone still in).
  3. Collect every PC's exam log: sudo primers-fleet collect ~/exam-logs/2026-12-03-csc101 (use the date and course).
  4. Check: sudo primers-fleet status shows the exam off everywhere.
  5. Copy the exam-logs folder to the exam officer's USB stick. It is personal data: keep it safe, delete it when no longer needed.
  6. Hand out the feedback forms.
Never: start an exam from a lab PC; tell anyone the staff password; switch off the invigilator's PC during the exam.   Incidents: seat, time, what happened, what you did; on the back of this card.
